## Not verified, and things to know

- Chrome only. Safari and Firefox were not run. The browser emulated reduced motion, so the sync meter's draw-in and other motion were not exercised.
- The browser journeys use SQLite. PostgreSQL and MySQL behaviour was not exercised here (PHPUnit covers SQLite and the other agents' work covers the rest).
- The real InfoDot redeem endpoint was not called: criterion 12 uses the fake client behind a test-only flag. The Origin check, the provisioning, the team sync, the session and the polling endpoint are the real code.
- New listings reach other people's Floor at the next `pulse:match` run (every ten minutes in production). The journeys run that job by hand (`Journey.tick`); search and the pause/archive/off paths do not wait for it.
- A match already seen and off screen keeps its old reasons after "Stop using this" until the next matching run (existing design, tested in `FloorTest`); beats on screen are redrawn at once.
- A beat counts as "seen" when the browser reports it on screen, so the journeys scroll through a round before asserting "caught up".
- `/dev/login` and `/dev/logout` are registered only under local and testing; that they are absent in production is by the `if (app()->environment(...))` guard and was not tested in a process booted as production.
- Mail delivery (digest, notification e-mails), file uploads (logos, photos) and the operator desk were not driven by these specs (agent B and the other agents cover criteria 9, 10, 13 to 20).
- In this environment port 8781 was taken by another project's server, so the runner moves to the next free port (and `PULSE_E2E_PORT` overrides it).
- `npm run build` is needed for the two CSS fixes to show (`public/build` is not tracked; the deploy builds it).
